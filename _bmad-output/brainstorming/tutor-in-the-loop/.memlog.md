- (technique) started Role Storming (Tutor Perspective)
- (idea by coach) Tutor Curation Gate: AI homework is saved as DRAFT; Tutor customizes questions, adds personal notes, and explicitly approves before publishing to student
- (direction by user) Adding/editing/deleting AI questions is already in the current design; need deeper tutor-in-the-loop innovations
- (idea by coach) Tutor Spaced-Repetition Control Dashboard: SM-2 algorithm triggers Forgetting Curve Alerts to Tutor instead of auto-assigning to student. Tutor decides to 1-click approve 5-min review, merge into next lesson warm-up, or snooze.
- (idea by coach) Tutor Pedagogical Strategy Injector: Tutor specifies teaching strategy (Scaffolding, Error-Targeted, Interest-Based) to guide AI homework generation, ensuring homework reflects the tutor's unique pedagogy.
- (insight by user) Tutor role in AI generation needs to be deeper than just strategy picker; Spaced Repetition needs adaptation for 1-2 sessions/week tutor model
- (idea by coach) Between-Session Micro-Intervention: Spaced Repetition fills the 3-4 day gap between weekly tutor sessions. Tutor sends mid-week 3-question review with personal feedback, creating continuous tutor engagement.
- (idea by coach) Tutor Raw-Material Injector: Homework is generated from Tutor post-class voice/text notes (specific classroom interactions) merged with SKP, producing homework impossible for self-study apps.
- (insight by user) Mid-week homework creates student friction; mandatory post-class context entry causes high tutor friction (tutors are lazy/busy)
- (idea by coach) Tutor-Led In-Class Warm-up Spaced Repetition: SM-2 automatically builds a 5-minute Entrance Ticket/Warm-up for the START of the next tutoring session (0-click for tutor, zero friction mid-week). Tutor runs it live in class.
- (idea by coach) Zero-Friction AI Homework with Tutor Pedagogical Guide: AI auto-generates homework from curriculum lesson + SKP without manual tutor input, and provides Tutor with a Teaching & Remediation Guide for next class.
- (insight by user) AI generating difficulty & tags up front wastes tokens and gets stale if tutor edits question text; forcing tutors to manually tag non-AI questions adds high friction
- (idea by coach) Just-In-Time / On-Publish Auto-Tagging: AI generates/edits raw question text first without metadata; upon tutor 'Publish', a fast micro-model generates difficulty & tags on the FINAL text in 1 bulk call for both AI and manual questions.
- (idea by coach) Scope Inheritance Strategy: Questions created inside a Lesson/SKP module automatically inherit topic tags and target CEFR level from the parent Lesson, removing the need per-question AI or manual tagging.
- (idea by coach) Empirical Student Data Calibration: Difficulty score starts as a soft estimate (or word-length heuristic) and is dynamically updated based on actual student accuracy/time performance over time, eliminating AI guessing costs.
- (insight by user) Implicit AI auto-tagging on Publish creates confusion/dissatisfaction if tutors see token quota deducted without explicitly asking for AI assistance.
- (idea by coach) Explicit AI Assist Button with Transparent Cost: Auto-tagging is an OPTIONAL explicit action ('AI Auto-Tag & Score - Costs 1 Credit'). Otherwise, default free Scope Inheritance is used upon Publish.
- (idea by coach) Zero-Token Rule-Based NLP & Scope Inheritance: Tagging uses parent Lesson tags (Free) + rule-based keyword matching (Free). Difficulty uses readability & word-length heuristics (Free), completely bypassing LLM token consumption.
- (question by user) Evaluating Option A difficulty model: Is 1-100 scale dataset preparation too complex? Is CEFR level mapping (A1-C2) better or problematic?
- (insight by coach) CEFR (A1-C2) is far superior to 1-100 scale: CEFR has open-source dictionaries/grammars for zero-token rule-based matching, and is intuitively understood by tutors.
- (idea by coach) Hybrid CEFR + ELO Dynamic Scoring: Static CEFR band (A1-C2) parsed 0-token via vocabulary/grammar dictionaries + dynamic ELO rating adjusted automatically based on student pass/fail rates.
- (decision by user) ELO Rating Logic Clarification: Student vs Question match - when Student answers correctly, Student ELO increases and Question difficulty ELO decreases (and vice versa).
- (decision by user) FINAL DECISION: When requesting AI to generate exercises, AI will directly output topic tags and CEFR difficulty levels (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2).
